This library lives on a small computer that broadcasts its own WiFi. **There is no internet here** — everything you see is stored on the device itself, so it keeps working when phone networks and the internet are down.

> Anyone within WiFi range can connect, read, and download. Nothing is tracked. Please tell others it's here.

## If you are in danger right now

Go straight to the **Emergency** cards on the home page:

- Severe bleeding → **Bleeding Control**
- Someone not breathing → **First Aid Priorities**
- No safe water → **Making Water Safe**
- Cold, wet, or night coming → **The First 72 Hours** and **Shelter Basics**

## What is here

| Section | What it gives you |
|---|---|
| Library | Practical guides: water, fire, shelter, first aid, food, navigation, signals, tools, transport, hazards, community |
| Compass | Phone compass (when the browser allows sensors) and a **sun compass that needs no sensors** — only the clock and your rough location |
| Maps | World overview offline; detailed regional maps if the operator loaded them; tap to read coordinates, measure distances, save waypoints |
| 3D Prints | Printable files for hand tools, water gear, navigation instruments, pulleys, gears, winches and cart wheels — with how to print and use each one |
| Toolkit | Morse code signaller, SOS flasher, water-bleach calculator, CPR metronome, pace counter |
| Take It With You | A single-file copy of every article for your phone, and the whole library to start another hotspot |

## Take a copy

Your phone may lose the connection when you walk away. Open **Take It With You** and save the single-file copy — it opens in any browser with no network. Then share it: by Bluetooth, USB stick, or by setting up another hotspot node.

## A note on safety

These guides are written for situations where professional help is unavailable. When a doctor, paramedic, engineer or local expert is available, use them. Pick the safest option you can, go slowly, and look after each other.
