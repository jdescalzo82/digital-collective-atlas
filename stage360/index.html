Every Seat

A five-piece band plays “Every Angle” in a virtual theatre. Watch the director’s guided cut, or pick your own seat and look anywhere in 360°.

  • Headphones give you true binaural sound that turns as you look.
  • Pick a seat from the overhead seating chart in your program: four audience seats and two backstage cameras.
  • Captions, audio description and a hands-free mode are in Settings.

On Meta Quest or Apple Vision Pro, open this page in the headset browser to watch in VR.

Program · Notes

About tonight

Every Seat is an experimental tool on Toolkit Your Life. A five-piece band plays “Every Angle” in a virtual theatre, standing in for real 360° camera feeds. It’s built for people who can’t easily get to a venue.

Your own 360 footage

The clip replaces the virtual theatre for that camera and plays its own audio, synced to the show clock.
